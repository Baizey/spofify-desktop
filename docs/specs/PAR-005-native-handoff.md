## Useful delivery / contract

Receive a current browser's ordinary full prepared queue, actually acquire its uncached media at nonzero position/time, play/pause correctly, and transfer it back without losing musical identity. This upgrades a usable local queue into an interoperable receiver; it is neither a DTO-only promise nor priority advertisement. D `player.rs::Endpoint/consume/valid_snapshot`, `audio.rs::activate/execute`, `engine.rs` async continuations; G `playback.go`, `playback_dto.go`, `playback_test.go::TestPlaybackTransferRoundTripExactContinuity` and `playback_quarantine_test.go`.

- Accept/validate complete v1 state with queue keys/order, position, context, shuffle/repeat, truncation/notices, manual entry IDs, derived target/occurrence IDs and current history event/progress/reported. Restore the exact current occurrence, not index zero or first matching track ID. Import prepared contents directly; do not refetch mutable playlists or persist signed selection tokens.
- `prepare` is validation only: no replacing queue, media acquisition or audible side effects. `release` fences/silences and cancels all source continuations while ACKing final exact state with preserved playing intent. Authorized `activate` performs cancellable asynchronous media acquisition/seek and publishes actual final `ok/blocked/media/rejected` state in the same poll as exact-delivery/epoch ACK. Poll/lease maintenance continues during work. Complete within coordinator's 8-second operation budget or fail/fence honestly; no fake ready ACK followed by unguarded later start.
- Handle activation even without locally observed prepare according to canonical validation. Paused transfer remains paused with exact current/time; if bytes are acquired lazily, explicit Resume must obtain and seek them safely. Incoming remote play/playCollection/enqueue/enqueueContext, transport, seek and keyed queue edits execute against the same receiver model; keep legacy command meanings, not priority substitutions.
- Enforce source/session/request/epoch/monotonic lease and per-sample fences through every await. Delayed/lost release/activate ACK, newer target, authority ABA, sign-out and closed window cannot restart. ACK dedup and final-state atomicity survive asynchronous completion. Unknown outcome drains last renewed lease; source-only loss may recover the selected destination **paused**, requester/target loss cancels, no spontaneous reselect/replay on GET/reconnect.
- Preserve `clear` upcoming-only and incoming `close` reset semantics. **No user-facing Close player, reset Stop, Stop listening/dismiss or now-playing popup.** Retry is real local native-media/output recovery, not browser autoplay fiction. Do not advertise `queueIntentsVersion:1` yet; capable→native legacy hop may lose priority metadata by protocol, never musical state/occurrence.
